BASSLINE YF-21 - Linux (x86_64)
===============================

VST3 PLUGIN
  Copy the "Bassline YF-21.vst3" folder to   ~/.vst3/
    mkdir -p ~/.vst3 && cp -r "Bassline YF-21.vst3" ~/.vst3/
  Then rescan plugins in your DAW (Bitwig, Reaper, Ardour, Carla...).

STANDALONE
  ./"Bassline YF-21"     (if needed first:  chmod +x "Bassline YF-21")
  Click "Options" (top-left) -> "Audio/MIDI Settings..." to choose ALSA or
  JACK, your output device and your MIDI input.
  - JACK / PipeWire-JACK: start your JACK server (or use  pw-jack ./"Bassline YF-21").

REQUIREMENTS
  Needs the usual desktop libraries: ALSA (libasound2), fontconfig, freetype.
  JACK is optional and loaded only if installed.

PATCHES AND PATTERNS
  ~/.config/Bassline YF-21/Patches   and   ~/.config/Bassline YF-21/Patterns
