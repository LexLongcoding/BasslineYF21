BASSLINE YF-21 - Windows (64-bit)
=================================

VST3 PLUGIN
  1. Copy the whole "Bassline YF-21.vst3" folder to:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
     (Windows will ask for administrator permission - click Continue.)
  2. In your DAW, rescan plugins. Bassline YF-21 appears as an instrument.

STANDALONE
  Double-click "Bassline YF-21.exe" - no install needed.
  Click "Options" (top-left) -> "Audio/MIDI Settings..." to choose your audio
  output and tick your keyboard / controller under "Active MIDI inputs".

  Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the app
  isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click "More info" ->
  "Run anyway". This only happens once.

PATCHES AND PATTERNS
  Saved patches:   %APPDATA%\Bassline YF-21\Patches
  Saved patterns:  %APPDATA%\Bassline YF-21\Patterns
  (paste into the Explorer address bar). The Standalone and the VST3 share them.
